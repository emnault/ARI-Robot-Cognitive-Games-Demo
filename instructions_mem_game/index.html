Card Matching Game: Instructions

We are going to play a game of pairs with twenty red playing cards, Ace through ten. We will take turns to turn over any two cards. If both are a pair, for example, both are number fives, then that same player has another turn. If the cards are not a pair, then the other player takes a turn. We will continue to do this until the whole deck has been turned over. Try to remember where cards are. You should improve with practice. The winner is the player with the most cards at the end of the game!
